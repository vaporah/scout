The important questions

Clear on what Scout does.

Does Scout trade for me?

No. Scout does not connect a wallet, sign transactions or place orders.

Does a green highlight mean a coin is safe?

No. It reflects available research evidence, which can be incomplete and can change quickly.

What happens to my data?

Settings stay local. Public market providers receive requests for the token information shown in the product. Read the privacy policy.
