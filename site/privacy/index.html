Privacy policy

What the Scout browser extension stores, what it sends, and what it never does.

Last updated 15 October 2026 · applies to the Scout extension and this website

The short version. Scout is a research tool. It reads public market data about Solana tokens and shows it beside the coins you are already looking at. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. The optional live market panel on this website makes a public request to GeckoTerminal; everything Scout itself remembers is stored on your own computer.

What Scout stores on your device

All of it is local. None of it is transmitted to the publisher or to anyone else.

StoredWhy
Your settings: score threshold, minimum liquidity, trade size, slippageSo the extension behaves the way you configured it
Pinned mint addressesSo the coins you chose to track survive a reload
Platform and quote-asset filtersSo the popup and the dashboard agree
Panel size, position and collapsed stateSo the popup stays where you put it
Coins Scout has seen, and their observed highsTo tell whether a coin is a repeat, and what its high was

Uninstalling the extension, or clearing site data for axiom.trade or gmgn.ai, removes all of it.

What Scout sends over the network

Scout makes read-only GET requests for public market data. There are no uploads, no form posts, and no cookies or credentials are attached to any request. Requests go only to the hosts listed in the extension's permissions:

HostWhat it is asked for
api.dexscreener.comPool, price, liquidity and volume
api.geckoterminal.comPool data, holder distribution, all-time high
api.gopluslabs.ioToken security and holder list
api.rugcheck.xyzToken report, holder accounts, deploying wallet
gmgn.aiHolder count, concentration and wallet-quality figures

The only information in those requests is a public Solana mint address: the coin being looked at. No identifier for you, your browser, your settings or your holdings is included.

Be aware of the obvious consequence: as with any request to a third party, those services see your IP address and the fact that this coin was queried. That is inherent to fetching public data on the internet, and their own privacy policies govern how they handle it.

Live market panel on scoutai.trade

The homepage's live Solana trending-pools panel requests the top three pools from api.geckoterminal.com when the homepage is open, then refreshes no more than once per minute. It sends no wallet address, settings, token search, account data, or identifier. Like every public web request, GeckoTerminal receives your IP address. The panel is optional presentation data and is not a trading signal.

The panel shows each token's published logo, so the browser also fetches those images from coin-images.coingecko.com and assets.geckoterminal.com. Those two are the only image hosts the page is permitted to load, and they see the same thing any image host does: your IP address and which logo was asked for. If a logo is missing or blocked, the row falls back to the ticker's first letter.

What Scout reads on the page

Scout's content scripts run on axiom.trade and gmgn.ai only. To match a coin row to a token, it reads the coin rows on the page: the symbol, the mint address in links or data attributes, and the visible market figures already shown to you. It does not read form fields, keystrokes, clipboard contents, other tabs, or any page outside those two sites.

What Scout never does

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage statistics.
  • No account, sign-in or identifier of any kind.
  • No selling, sharing or transmitting of data about you.
  • No remote code. All scripts are packaged in the extension, and the content security policy is script-src 'self'.
  • No trading. Scout never connects a wallet, never signs anything, and never places an order or moves funds. It only displays information.

Changes
